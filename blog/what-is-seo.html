What Is SEO? A Simple Guide for Business Owners

By Abdul Qadeer · AQadeer GEO.Com · SEO practice article

SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) means improving your website so it appears higher in Google search results when people search for what you sell — without paying for every click, the way ads work.

How Google decides who ranks first

Google wants to show the most helpful, trustworthy page for each search. It looks at hundreds of signals, but for a small business the big ones are simple:

  • Relevance — does your page actually answer the search?
  • Content quality — is it clear, complete and genuinely useful?
  • Technical health — does the page load fast, work on mobile and get indexed?
  • Trust — do other good websites and happy customers mention you?

The 4 parts of SEO

1. Keyword research

Finding the exact words your customers type into Google — for example “best dentist in Lahore” or “restaurant menu design” — and building one page for each important topic.

2. On-page SEO

Writing good title tags, headings and content on each page, and linking your pages together so Google understands your site. See my on-page SEO checklist.

3. Technical SEO

Speed, mobile-friendliness, sitemaps, robots.txt and fixing errors so Google can crawl and index everything.

4. Off-page SEO

Reviews, listings and links from other websites that prove your business is real and trusted.

How long does SEO take?

SEO is not instant. Most sites see early movement in 1–2 months on easy keywords and strong results in 3–6 months. That is slower than ads — but once you rank, the traffic keeps coming without paying per click. If your site gets no traffic at all, read why your website is not getting traffic from Google.

Red flags when hiring SEO help

  • Guaranteed #1 ranking in a few days — impossible to promise honestly.
  • No report of what was actually done each month.
  • Thousands of cheap links — these can get your site penalised.
  • They will not explain their work in simple language.
