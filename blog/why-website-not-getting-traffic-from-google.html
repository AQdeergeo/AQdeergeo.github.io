Why Your Website Is Not Getting Traffic from Google

By Abdul Qadeer · AQadeer GEO.Com · SEO practice article

Your website is live, but Google sends almost nobody. This is one of the most common problems small businesses face — and it usually has one of these fixable causes.

1. Google has not indexed your pages

If a page is not in Google’s index, it cannot rank at all. Check by searching site:yourwebsite.com on Google. Fix: submit your sitemap in Google Search Console and make sure robots.txt is not blocking your pages. My free Robots.txt Generator helps you build a safe file.

2. Your content answers nobody’s search

Pages written only about “us” — our company, our vision — do not match what customers search. Each page needs one clear topic and one target keyword. Start with what SEO is, then plan content around real questions.

3. Thin or copied content

Very short pages, or text copied from other sites, give Google no reason to rank you. Write original, helpful content — even 600–900 focused words beat 200 generic ones.

4. Slow, mobile-unfriendly pages

Most searches happen on phones. If your site is slow or hard to use on mobile, visitors leave and rankings fall. Compress images and test on your own phone.

5. No internal links and no fresh content

Pages that nothing links to are hard for Google to find and trust. Link your blog posts to your service pages, and publish helpful content regularly — consistency beats one big burst.

6. You are simply new

A brand-new website needs time. Google trusts older, proven sites first. Keep publishing, keep fixing, and measure with Search Console instead of guessing.
