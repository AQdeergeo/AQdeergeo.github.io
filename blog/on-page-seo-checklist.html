On-Page SEO Checklist (The One I Use on Every Page)

By Abdul Qadeer · AQadeer GEO.Com · SEO practice article

This is the exact checklist I follow on this website. Use it for every page or blog post you publish. You can also tick these items live on my SEO practice page.

Before writing

  • One target keyword chosen — a phrase real customers search.
  • Search intent clear: is the searcher learning, comparing or buying?
  • Competitor pages checked: what do the top results cover?

Title & meta

  • Title tag 50–60 characters, keyword near the start, written to earn the click. Test yours in the free Meta Tag Generator and SERP Preview tool.
  • Meta description 150–160 characters: benefit + call to action.
  • URL short and readable, with the keyword in it.

Content & structure

  • Only one H1. H2/H3 headings in logical order.
  • Keyword used naturally in the first 100 words — never stuffed. Check with the Keyword Density Checker.
  • Helpful, original content that fully answers the search.
  • Images compressed, with descriptive alt text and file names.

Links & technical

  • 2–5 internal links to related pages, with clear anchor text.
  • At least one useful external link where it genuinely helps.
  • Schema markup added where suitable — try the free Schema Generator.
  • Page loads fast on mobile; added to sitemap; indexed in Search Console.
